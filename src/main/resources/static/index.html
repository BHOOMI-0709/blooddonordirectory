General donation requirements

  • Age between 18 and 65 years
  • Weight of at least 45 kg
  • Hemoglobin usually 12.5 g/dL or higher
  • Gap since last donation: about 3 months for men, 4 months for women
  • You should feel healthy on the day, with no fever or infection

These are general guidelines. The final decision is always made by a doctor or blood bank after screening.

Frequently asked questions

Is a donor's phone number visible to everyone?

Yes, the whole point of the directory is direct contact. That is why we ask for your consent when you register. You can ask an admin to remove your entry at any time.

What does "Rest period" mean?

The donor gave blood recently and needs time to recover. Until then the card shows "Rest period".

What happens when a request is fulfilled?

An admin marks it as "Fulfilled" so people don't contact the requester unnecessarily.

What should I do first in an emergency?

Contact the nearest hospital or blood bank first. In India you can also call the emergency number 112. This directory is a helpful tool, not a replacement for medical verification.
